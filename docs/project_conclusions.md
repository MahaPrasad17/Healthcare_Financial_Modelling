## 5. Key Operational Risks & Mitigating Strategies

| Key Risk Factor | Impact on Investment Thesis | Strategic Mitigating Strategy |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Regulatory Diagnostic Price Caps** | Government price ceiling on routine pathology tests (e.g. CBC, Lipid, Dengue). | **High Genomics Insulation:** 49% of AuraGen's gross profit is generated from specialized NGS and clinical research, which are shielded from routine NLEM price caps. |
| **Sequencer Technology Obsolescence** | Rapid lifecycle transitions from Illumina NovaSeq to ultra-long-read sequencing (e.g. Oxford Nanopore, PacBio). | **Reagent Rental Model:** AuraGen structures sequencer procurement through reagent rental agreements with guaranteed multi-year upgrade pathways, reducing Capex risk. |
| **Doctor Referral / B2B Attrition** | Loss of hospital network ties post-acquisition. | **Direct Patient Collection:** Rapidly growing collection center network (280 centers) shifts business to direct patient B2C footfall, insulating against referral churn. |
| **Key Talent Retention (Bioinformaticians)** | Loss of senior geneticists and bioinformatic variant curation teams. | **Retention Equity Pools:** Deal structuring allocates a 3-year performance earnout pool for key molecular pathologists and scientific leadership. |
